Who We Are

Crafting Quality Aluminum Solutions

JEX Aluminum is a professional aluminum fabrication company based in Enugu State, Nigeria. We specialize in the manufacturing and installation of high-quality aluminum windows, doors, office partitions, and custom aluminum structures.

Over the years, we have worked with homeowners, businesses, and construction companies to deliver durable and modern aluminum solutions that improve both functionality and aesthetics.

Our goal is simple — to provide reliable aluminum installations that combine quality materials, skilled craftsmanship, and excellent customer service. Every project we take on, big or small, receives the same level of care and attention.
